Initial release of AsistenciaBLE (v1.0.0):
• Serverless offline attendance via Bluetooth Low Energy
• Rotating HMAC-SHA256 tokens and 64-position bitmap echo
• Course roster import (.xlsx, .csv, clipboard)
• One-tap inverted exception list for students without phones
• QR enrollment with X25519 key exchange
• 4-sheet Excel (.xlsx) export and signed session minutes
• Optional Google Sheets/Drive backup
• Animated walkthrough and help center
• Two flavors: Teacher and Student
